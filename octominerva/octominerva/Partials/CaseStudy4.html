A very large local authority contracted Octominerva to design and deliver a 20 workshop programme to senior managers, based on the working title, 'dealing with change'.

It became very clear, very quickly that the managers were extremely adroit at dealing with change; they saw and felt its effects on a daily basis. What we focused on was the need to change the terminology to 'transitions' and to remember that when you are asking people to transit to a different place with you, fun and innovation are key. The perfect example of a successful method of effective behavioural change, we were able to illustrate to this client how they had to cease the diktat method of piling change programmes on top of each other.
