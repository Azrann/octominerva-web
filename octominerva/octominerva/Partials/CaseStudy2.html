Wake from the sleep of habit and view your organisation from a fresh perspective

When Octominerva was asked to facilitate a series of sessions as part of a nine day residential programme, we were asked to design a two hour session entitled, "Commercial Awareness". Hardly an inspiring title so we took the view that as these were Directors of private, public and voluntary organisations, we would approach this how the founder of the modern essay, Montaigne, might have done.

Using as its foundation a wonderful book by Sarah Bakewell, we adapted her work to provide twenty answers to the question of "how to live", all in the spirit of Montaigne. Our premise was that by using Montaigne's writings, our delegates would be able to view their organisations from a different perspective, "wake from the sleep of habit" and place whatever they did that made them feel proud at the heart of their business plans.
