Cookies

The Octominerva website uses cookies, and by using this website you are consenting to their use in accordance with the terms of this policy. Cookies are files sent by web servers to web browsers, where they are stored. This information is sent back to the server each time a page is requested by the browser, enabling the server to identify and track them.

The two main cookie types are session cookies and persistent cookies. Session cookies are deleted from your computer when you close your browser, whereas persistent cookies remain stored on your computer until deleted, or until they reach their expiry date.

Octominerva uses Google Analytics to analyse the use of this website. Google Analytics generates statistical and other information about website use by means of cookies, which are stored on users' computers. The information generated relating to our website is used to create reports about the use of the website. Google will store and use this information. You may read Google's privacy policy here.

Most browsers will allow you to refuse to accept cookies. If you are using Internet Explorer, you may refuse all cookies by clicking ‘Tools’, ‘Internet Options’, and ‘Privacy’, and selecting ‘Block all cookies’. In Firefox, you may adjust your cookies settings by clicking “Tools”, “Options” and “Privacy”.

Blocking cookies may have a negative impact upon the usability of some websites.
