Building better relationships with the help of Caravaggio's Cardsharps



An executive team, riven by in-fighting, aggressive, time precious and unwillingly to admit to their long-standing problems. Previous consultancy interventions had ended in various states of disrepair, blame, contractual wrangling, and no end of pain. Our approach was simple; slow things down, avoid even the merest hint of 'teaching' any content whatsoever and with the lightest possible touch to facilitate some genuine conversations.

This was a whole day session with one solitary image used; Caravaggio's Cardsharps (1595). By giving an interpretation of what was happening in the picture, we could open up the dialogue between the team using this allegory. A tense but hugely rewarding day’s session which allowed that team to begin to flourish once more.
