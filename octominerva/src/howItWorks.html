How it works


There is no set pattern, no template, no formula and there is certainly no one ‘right solution’. Instead, projects build, sometimes slowly, sometimes with amazing rapidity, from conversations and ideas.

At the first contact stage we make it our sole purpose to listen to you; to hear and sense the prevailing issues that you are experiencing as an individual / group / team / organisation. Together we then craft a session or series of sessions around this. The one common thread across the entire Octominerva portfolio is that these facilitated sessions are all emergent and bespoke. This dialogic way of working allows you and us to be flexible, acting in the moment, but ensuring that the impacts are wide ranging and long lasting.

The nuance in the above comes with Executive Coaching, a confidential, one-to-one space in which patterns of behaviour and emerging emotions can be explored safely. We believe Executive Coaching sessions can be immensely powerful, are an essential element of any series of sessions or long term project, and also stand proudly on their own.

Our coaching practice is person-centred and relational, with all sessions being completely confidential, exploratory and free flowing. Through the use of propitious questions and sensory awareness, our aim is to cultivate a rich dialogue and to provide a safe haven; one that allows coachees to sit at the feet of their experience.
