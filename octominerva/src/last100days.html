What impact has the last one hundred days had on you...?



When working on residential programmes, how do you reconnect a group of delegates when three months have passed between modules and everyday 'stuff' has got in the way? The Octominerva solution? Find a fabulous still photograph taken somewhere on the planet each day in the intervening period, place a suitably evocative soundtrack to the images, darken the room and roll the images on a big screen.

By letting the delegates emerge themselves in this sumptuous audio/visual experience, the group not only connects with their wider world view, but also each other.
