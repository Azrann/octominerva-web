The story of Octominerva


Founded 6 years ago, Octominerva is based on the West Coast of Scotland and is led by Gareth L. Smith, a road cycling obsessive who resides nestled at the foot of the hills, 500m from the sea. Here is his attempt to capture Octominerva’s essence:

Some things I hold dear; hilltop vistas, the presence of the sea, fine coffee, art, literature, whimsy, eloquence, verisimilitude and Nordic noir. I have gained a reputation for being able to facilitate Strategy Away Days for Boards of Directors / Executive Teams, where mixing art, literature, and sport with clean, crisp questions; light touch facilitation; and a blank agenda, brings about tangible and meaningful results. The route I take to achieve this may not be conventional but is always stimulating, never far from insightful, and often deeply meaningful. I love to work with the concept of organisational sense-making, where we can usually find a significant majority who recognise themselves in Kafka’s ”plight of the individual caught up in the remorseless logic of an irrational system”. But also sense-shaping; awakening the senses and raising awareness through art, literature or simply being sensitively present in the moment.

Put grandly, I have an aspiration in all I do to cultivate an aesthetic appreciation of everything that matters, while helping others to seek out beauty, poise, clarity and intelligence. Never forgetting of course that ”if we cannot remember it all, then we should at least try to have an idea of what we have forgotten” (Clive James).
