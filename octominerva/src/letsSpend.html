Let's spend a couple of hours talking about a bloke in Lycra


Octominerva was asked to facilitate a series of sessions for a group of senior managers at a nationwide organisation. The remit here was twofold; explore what it takes to be a high performing team and then to provide support on how to apply this to various teams. By using the examples of Mark Cavendish and his HTC team sprint team, the notion of being a 'domestique' and serving others was applied.

What role did each of the team play? What were all the scenarios where roles had to change? All leading to the counterintuitive point that ultimately it's not about what you can contribute, it's what you are prepared to sacrifice to be a member of your community.

Months later we are still receiving thanks from many of the various teams, all saying how that session and thinking not only unlocked all of the unhelpful patterns they had become stuck in but also ensured they realised their true potential.
